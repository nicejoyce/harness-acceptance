# Global Rules

- Completion claims require actual output, reports, or artifact digests under `DOC-001` and `DOC-006`.
- External inputs, secrets, error handling, resources, and authorization follow `SEC-*`, `DATA-*`, and `BOUND-*`.
- Default to composition, directional dependencies, and testable domain logic under `ARCH-*`.
- Stack rules and project profile may add detail but cannot weaken the baseline.
- Obtain approval before irreversible, production, network-side-effect, or sensitive-data operations.
