# Document Hierarchy

When instructions conflict, apply the highest applicable source and document the resolution.

1. Law, regulation, contractual obligations, and platform policy.
2. Incident commander or security owner direction during an active incident.
3. Repository security policy and explicit user authorization.
4. This harness's safety and data policies.
5. Approved architecture, requirements, and quality-gate contracts.
6. Team workflow and coding conventions.
7. Implementation notes and convenience instructions.

## Records of Authority

- Product intent: requirement record.
- Ordinary defect lifecycle and history: bug tracker.
- Cross-work-item delivery state: project progress.
- Security, data, and material operational response: incident record.
- Technical trade-off: ADR.
- Material behavior or architecture: design record.
- Known risk or exception: risk assessment and approved exception.
- Verification: test plan and actual command output.
- Operational release: release record.

No lower-priority document may authorize production access, credential use, security bypass, destructive action, or third-party side effect.
