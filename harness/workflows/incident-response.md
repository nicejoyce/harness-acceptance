# Incident Response Workflow

1. Confirm an incident owner and severity; start `templates/incident-record.md`.
2. Contain safely: revoke exposed credentials, stop unsafe automation, restrict access, and preserve relevant evidence.
3. Do not publish secrets, customer data, exploit details, or unverified root-cause claims.
4. Escalate security, privacy, legal, and platform-policy concerns to their assigned owners.
5. Recover through an approved runbook; verify service, data integrity, and security controls.
6. Record timeline, impact, root cause, corrective actions, and owners. Track corrective actions through normal delivery gates.
