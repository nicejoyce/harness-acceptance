## Operating Rules

- Update this record when a project phase, milestone, overall status, material risk, blocker, dependency, release readiness, or delivery forecast changes; refresh it at least once per reporting cycle.
- Every status claim links to current evidence. Do not call work complete, on track, or released when its required verification is missing.
- Keep work-item details in the authoritative requirement, bug, incident, and release records; summarize and link here instead of duplicating their histories.
